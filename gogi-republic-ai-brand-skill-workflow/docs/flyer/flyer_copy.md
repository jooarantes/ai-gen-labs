## Delivery notes

- This is the promotional flyer exercise, prompt 2 of the practice using prompts 1 through 3.
- The RSVP email opens a new email draft; it does not send automatically.
- Thursday is intentionally not expanded to a calendar date. No street address or additional offer conditions were supplied.
- Typography uses locally available condensed heading, sans-serif body, and monospace label fallbacks. The page needs no external fonts or scripts.
- Responsive screen layout and a single-page US Letter print layout are included.
- All source images are reused without editing or regeneration.
