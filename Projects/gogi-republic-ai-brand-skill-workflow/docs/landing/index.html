01 / The food

Make room.
Then make ssam.

Start with the sizzle. Reach for the kimchi. Keep the good stuff moving around the table.

Meats
Galbi, pork belly, brisket, spicy chicken, and ribeye cap. Bring an appetite for char.

Banchan
